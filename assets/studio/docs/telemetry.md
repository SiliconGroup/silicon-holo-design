# Telemetry channels

| Channel | Rate | Unit |
|---------|-----:|------|
| attitude | 48 Hz | quaternion |
| power | 4 Hz | watt |
| thermal | 1 Hz | kelvin |

Formulae are rendered by the Markdown renderer, including math:

$$
q_{t+1} = q_t + \frac{1}{2} \Omega(\omega_t)\, q_t \, \Delta t
$$

Inline math such as $E = mc^2$ is supported too.
